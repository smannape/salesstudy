### Detail

- **McKinsey:** 70% of digitisation projects have not moved beyond pilot; failures trace to unclear value, weak adoption, and siloed projects not integrated with IT/OT; upstream digital value >$5/boe; companies applying all four influence-model elements are 8x more likely to meet change objectives ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/harnessing-volatility-technology-transformation-in-oil-and-gas)).
- **Bain:** 75% of energy/natural-resources companies say digital fails to scale; about one in four moves beyond pilots. Fixes: plan scaling vectors from day one, value-first use-case selection, operating-model clarity, frontline operationalisation, modernise IT in parallel ([Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/scaling-digital-operations-in-energy-and-natural-resources/)).
- **Accenture:** only 7% of energy organisations have scaled AI enterprise-wide; 64% say employees trust human colleagues more than AI; "skills diffusion, not model accuracy" is the constraint; Aramco $1.8bn AI value in 2024, 442 use cases ([Accenture, Mar 2026](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/insights/energy/scaling-ai-upstream-energy)).
- **McKinsey via OGJ:** fewer than 20% of AI deployments track value and share the KPI. Barriers: incomplete data, legacy systems, excessive in-house development, underestimated change management, misaligned commercial models. "AI creates the most value when it changes decisions" ([OGJ on McKinsey, Aug 2026](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/news/55400981/analysis-ai-could-unlock-230-billion-in-annual-upstream-oil-and-gas-value)).
- **SPE:** <20% adoption after two decades of digital oilfield; >90% of E&C organisations fail to sustain adoption; change management ≈50% of hours in successful data-science projects; trained "pathfinders" raise change readiness up to sevenfold ([JPT change management](https://jpt.spe.org/dont-forget-people-your-digital-transformation-programs-change-management-essential-competency)).
- **GCC:** assets have "a good deal of autonomy"; only 7% of 200+ O&G companies were Digital Champions; recommend digital programme managers under a CDO ([Strategy&](https://www.strategyand.pwc.com/m1/en/articles/2020/the-digital-imperative-for-gcc-oil-and-gas-companies.html)).
- **KOC workshop:** small pilots in part of an asset are effective; main hurdle is "attitude and reluctance to leave comfort zones, especially among technical staff"; KOC ran three digital-oilfield pilots ([JPT Kuwait workshop](https://jpt.spe.org/workshop-kuwait-lifts-bar-digital-transformation-og)).
- **Baker Hughes:** employees under-reported AI gains for job-security reasons ([JPT ADIPEC 2024](https://jpt.spe.org/in-abu-dhabi-the-oil-and-gas-industry-gets-a-glimpse-of-the-future)).

## Drivers and blockers

<div class="panel">

<p class="eyebrow">Card</p>

### Driver / blocker card <span class="tag">inference</span>

**Drivers**

- Named value hypothesis with baseline and shared KPI.
- Asset-level executive sponsor.
- Pilot inside one asset with a pre-agreed scaling vector.
- Data residency answered up front (private/sovereign cloud): Petrobel on-prem "partly to meet data residency requirements" ([Halliburton](https://www.halliburton.com/en/about-us/press-release/petrobel-selects-halliburton-landmark-design-deliver-cloud-solutions-ep-applications)); Aramco Digital sovereign cloud ([TM Forum](https://inform.tmforum.org/features-and-opinion/aramco-digitals-tareq-amin-spells-out-platform-service-ambitions-for-the-middle-east/)).
- OSDU/open integration — OSDU Data Platform Standard v1 in 2026 ([Open Group](https://blog.opengroup.org/2025/12/04/osdu-forum-highlights-from-the-open-group-houston-summit-2025)).
- Funded change resources past go-live.

**Blockers**

- Procurement separating software from services.
- AI trust gap.
- Job-security fear.
- Headcount-rewarding contracts.
- Frameworks where vendor efficiency cuts billable scope.

## Pilot-to-scale route that works

PoC → multi-year multi-asset framework (AIQ-ADNOC: PoC then $340m 3-year award), not asset-by-asset re-tendering ([JPT](https://jpt.spe.org/aiq-announces-340-million-contract-from-adnoc-for-deployment-of-agentic-ai)).

- Agree the scaling vector (which assets, which users, which KPI) before the pilot starts (Bain).
- Keep the pilot inside one asset — the format KOC found effective (JPT Kuwait workshop).
- Publish the value in the NOC's own numbers and share the KPI (<20% do — OGJ/McKinsey).
- Fund change management past go-live; it is ≈50% of the effort in successful projects (JPT).
