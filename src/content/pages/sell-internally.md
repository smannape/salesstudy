## Champion test

- "Who gains the most personally and professionally from our solution succeeding?"
- "Can you help me arrange a brief meeting with [Economic Buyer]?" ([Hyperbound MEDDPICC](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/meddpicc-discovery-questions))
- Surface personal stake with Sandler personal-impact questions ([Hyperbound Sandler](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/sandler-pain-funnel)).
- MEDDPICC level-3 champion = active internal seller who shapes criteria and gives access. Red flag: champion cannot get you to EB or procurement ([Kalungi](https://www.kalungi.com/blog/using-meddpicc-inside-hubspot)).

## Executive business case template

Seven sections ([Mutiny](https://www.mutinyhq.com/blueprints/executive-business-case)):

<div class="checklist" data-checklist="exec-business-case">

- [ ] 1. Header — rep name/title + prospect logo
- [ ] 2. Executive summary — one-line headline + context paragraph
- [ ] 3. Strategic problem framing — problems, urgency, cost of inaction in the approver's terms
- [ ] 4. Recommended approach in phases
- [ ] 5. Target outcomes table — current vs target across 3–4 metrics
- [ ] 6. Required investment — prospect's and vendor's commitments side by side
- [ ] 7. Final recommendation with one clear ask

</div>

Tips: write for the budget holder; lead with cost of inaction; tie to payback period; build from the champion's own notes; use when the champion must take the decision to a VP/CFO who wasn't in the evaluation. Narrative spine = Command of the Message sequence ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/command-of-the-message)).

## Mutual Action Plan template

Source: [GetAccept](https://www.getaccept.com/blog/mutual-action-plans).

<div class="checklist" data-checklist="map-template">

- [ ] Overview and value statement tied to buyer priorities
- [ ] Success criteria / KPIs
- [ ] Stakeholder roles on both sides (Economic Buyer, Champion)
- [ ] 5–7 milestones, each with owner and due date

</div>

Best practices: co-create on a live call; name milestones by buyer outcomes; work backward from go-live; living document; extend to first value.

## Pre-wiring and regret reduction

- Agreeing the purchase objective early is "the single most effective way to reduce regret"; involve IT leadership and future users ([TechRepublic on Gartner](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/most-technology-purchases-come-with-high-degree-of-regret-gartner-says/)).
- Engage procurement earlier with business-impact data ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/28/forrester-b2b-buyers-demand-proof-not-promises/)).
- At approval stage address FOMU with safety nets, not more ROI slides ([Challenger Inc.](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)).
