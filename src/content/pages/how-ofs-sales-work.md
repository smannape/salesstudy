## The compliance stack

<span class="tag">inference</span> (from the cited sources below) — four gates precede any product conversation:

1. **Vendor registration / prequalification** on the NOC portal.
2. **Certified local-content score** scored in the commercial envelope.
3. **Cybersecurity certification.**
4. **Local entity or agent.**

**Two-envelope evaluation:** technical pass/fail → commercial with local-content weighting → lowest price. Technical differentiation only earns the right to compete on price.

## Kuwait / KOC

- **Public Tenders Law 49/2016:** purchases above **KD 75,000 (~$250k)** go through **CAPT**; CAPT invites prequalification and pre-tender meetings; oil-sector suppliers "must be approved by an internal committee and placed on a 'pre-approved' list"; tenders "usually awarded to the lowest price once technical compliance is established"; bidders can file grievances ([US Commercial Service Kuwait](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/kuwait-selling-public-sector)).
- **Local preference:** foreign bidders must buy ≥30% of materials locally where available (Article 87); domestic/GCC items get 15% preference; Kuwaiti businesses 10% price preference; performance bond 5–10% of contract value; many companies add 10–15% to costs for bond/project risk ([US Commercial Service Kuwait](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/kuwait-selling-public-sector); [Al Tamimi, new tender law](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/february-9/articles/the-impact-of-kuwaits-new-tender-law/)).
- **Agent rules:** Law 1/2024 amended Commercial Law Art. 24 and Tenders Law Art. 31 — foreign companies may open a branch and operate without a local agent, and are exempt from Kuwaiti commercial registration and agent requirements to bid and perform tenders ([Al Tamimi, foreign companies without agent](https://www.tamimi.com/news/foreign-companies-to-operate-in-kuwait-without-a-local-agent)).
- **Draft decree-law (Sept 2026):** on 9 Sept 2026 the Cabinet approved a draft decree-law amending Law 49/2016 that would prohibit local/commission agents in government contracting; **not in force** pending Gazette publication ([Deloitte Middle East](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/kuwait-cabinet-approves-draft-amendments-to-the-public-tenders-law.html)).
- **KPC subsidiaries** (KOC, KNPC, KOTC) run separate processes from CAPT and must be monitored directly ([OpenOpps Kuwait](https://openopps.com/blog/how-to-win-government-contracts-and-tenders-in-kuwait)).

## UAE / ADNOC

- **Registration** via SAP Ariba + SDM; categories Vendor, Manufacturer, Contractor, Associate, Agent; overseas manufacturers/associates need an Abu Dhabi agent (Exclusive or Representation agency); prequalification questionnaire covers capability, manpower, financial, quality, HSE ([ADNOC Supplier Registration guide 2023](https://adnoc.ae/-/media/adnoc-v2/files/suppliers/supplier-registration-and-prequalification-user-guide-updated24072023.ashx)).
- **ICV = 40% of the commercial evaluation;** no certificate = zero ICV score; certificate valid 14 months; issuance 3–15 business days ([Abu Dhabi Local Content FAQs](https://idb.added.gov.ae/Programs/ADLC/FAQs)).
- **Service-provider ICV scoring:** UAE third-party spend, UAE investment, Emiratisation, expat contribution (1–5 expats 1–3%; 6–50 4–6%; 51–200 7–9%; >200 10%), bonus up to 5%; agent bids average agent and principal ICV; gross negligence in ICV submissions can trigger a tender ban ([ADLC Unified ICV guideline](https://idb.added.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/IDB/ADLC/20200603-ADLC-Program-User-Guideline-Unifed-ICV.pdf)).
- ICV 3.0 is current ([Aviaan FAQ](https://aviaanaccounting.com/?p=19379) — third-party).
- **2026:** ADNOC Industrial Resilience Program, "Local+" and "ICV+" credit for purchases from local manufacturers; a localisation commitment is binding only if the contract says so ([Clyde & Co, June 2026](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/06/local-content-and-industrial-resilience-in-middle)).

## Saudi Arabia / Aramco

- **IKTVA ratio** = [(A localised goods/services + B Saudi salaries + C Saudi training + D supplier development + R R&D) / E revenue] + X export factor; baseline certified by an approved audit firm; 5-year IKTVA plan ([US Commercial Service, Aramco supplier](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/saudi-arabia-becoming-aramco-supplier)).
- Aramco targets retaining **75% of procurement spend in-Kingdom** ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/06/local-content-and-industrial-resilience-in-middle)).
- **Registration** <span class="tag danger">unverified</span> (consultancy summary): Saudi legal entity mandatory; Nitaqat Green/Platinum; SAP Ariba + IKTVA self-assessment; 8–16 weeks with an entity, 12–22 weeks from zero ([Analytix.sa](https://analytix.sa/aramco-vendor-registration/)).
- **Cybersecurity Compliance Certificate (CCC) under SACS-002:** CCC (self-assessment, remote) for general vendors and customised software; CCC+ (on-site) for network connectivity and critical data; both valid two years; firms include Baker Tilly, BDO, Crowe, Deloitte, Grant Thornton, KPMG, RSM, STC Solutions ([Consultancy-me](https://www.consultancy-me.com/news/3912/certification-firms-for-aramcos-third-party-cybersecurity-standard)); assessment 3–6 months ([Analytix.sa](https://analytix.sa/aramco-vendor-registration/)).

## OFS vs software selling

| Dimension | Pure enterprise software | OFS / Gulf NOC digital |
|---|---|---|
| Entry gate | Discovery call | Vendor registration, prequalification, pre-approved list ([US Commercial Service](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/kuwait-selling-public-sector)) |
| Scoring | Buyer-defined decision criteria | Technical compliance then lowest price; ICV 40% of commercial score at ADNOC ([ADLC FAQs](https://idb.added.gov.ae/Programs/ADLC/FAQs)) |
| Cyber | Infosec questionnaire late in paper process | Certification as hard gate (Aramco CCC/CCC+) ([Consultancy-me](https://www.consultancy-me.com/news/3912/certification-firms-for-aramcos-third-party-cybersecurity-standard)) |
| Local presence | Optional | Entity/agent historically required; Kuwait changing (Law 1/2024; 2026 draft decree) |
| Proof | Demo, trial | "Show me" demonstrations, site visits, in-person relationship work ([Geoffrey Cann / EnergyNow](https://energynow.ca/2020/12/transforming-oil-and-gas-sales-using-digital-geoffrey-cann/)) |
| Contract shape | SaaS order form | Multi-year framework bundling software + hosting + manpower (see [Revenue models](/revenue-models/)) |
| Risk | Churn | Bonds 5–10%, confiscable; 10–15% cost loading ([US Commercial Service](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/kuwait-selling-public-sector)) |
| Failure mode | No decision | Stalled deals, endless customisation, "we'll stick with Excel"; prebuild compliance features ([Nextworld](https://nextw.com/insights/navigating-saas-challenges-in-oil-gas-a-path-forward)) |

## Relationship playbook

<span class="tag">inference</span> (from cited deal histories):

1. **Land** — bounded PoC in one asset.
2. **Prove** — publish value in the NOC's own numbers.
3. **Expand** — multi-year, all-assets framework (KOC Feb→Jun 2021; ADNOC PoC→$340m).
4. **Embed** — operate the solution.
5. **Localise** — joint AI centre, national training (also scores ICV/IKTVA).
6. **Sponsor** — CEO-level quotes.
7. **Open** — OSDU/open architecture.

**Evidence:**

- Halliburton–KOC: North Kuwait Feb 2021, then West Kuwait / S&EK / Heavy Oil June 2021 ([Business Wire Feb 2021](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210203005051/en/Halliburton-to-Provide-Digital-Solutions-for-Kuwait-Oil-Company); [Business Wire June 2021](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210616005060/en/Kuwait-Oil-Company-Selects-Halliburton-for-Digital-Transformation-Projects)).
