## What lands (2025–26)

- Proven outcomes, not promises; "ROI data, customer references, and verified performance metrics"; business impact data, not features; engage procurement early ([Digital Commerce 360 on Forrester](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/28/forrester-b2b-buyers-demand-proof-not-promises/)).
- Tailored guidance, a "sounding board", consistent website/seller messaging ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience)).
- Case studies that explain HOW results were achieved; implementation and migration effort ([TechRepublic on Gartner](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/most-technology-purchases-come-with-high-degree-of-regret-gartner-says/)).

### Use / avoid

<div class="two-col">

<div>

**Use**

- "negative consequences", "positive business outcomes", "required capabilities", "metrics with baseline and target", "proof points" ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/command-of-the-message))
- "cost of inaction", "current state vs target state", "payback period", "required investment", "one clear ask" ([Mutiny](https://www.mutinyhq.com/blueprints/executive-business-case))
- Risk-reduction language: "realistic expectations", "safety net", "accountability" ([Challenger Inc.](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/))
- NOC-specific (from the [priority tiers](/long-term-strategy/)): "deployment-ready", "scaled", "OSDU-compliant", "sovereign/private cloud", "IT/OT integration", "production uplift", "digital twin", "national capability building"

</div>

<div>

**Avoid**

- "synergy", "paradigm shift", "game-changing", "disruptive", "cutting-edge", "innovative solution", "best-of-breed", "world-class", "low-hanging fruit", "passionate about delivering", "customer-centric"
- AI-generated tells: "In the rapidly evolving world of…", "let's delve into…"
- "AI-powered" / "machine learning at scale" — no longer differentiate
- 88% of buyers say buzzwords damage credibility ([The Insight Collective](https://www.theinsightcollective.com/insights/overused-b2b-buzzwords))
- "pilot" — say "deployment" <span class="tag">inference</span> from ADIPEC framing

</div>

</div>

## Rewrite example

- Before: "cutting-edge AI-powered analytics for world-class enterprises"
- After: "our analytics software helps retailers predict stockouts up to two weeks in advance, reducing lost sales by 20%" ([The Insight Collective](https://www.theinsightcollective.com/insights/overused-b2b-buzzwords))

## Red flag: AI-generated inaccuracy

19% of buyers lost confidence from inaccurate AI results; Forrester predicts a 2026 lawsuit over AI misrepresentation ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/28/forrester-b2b-buyers-demand-proof-not-promises/)).

## Conversation mechanics

- Longer buyer answers correlate with wins (peak ~50 words, rising toward 250).
- Mirror 1–3 key words, then pause.
- Ask questions "only an expert would ask" ([Gong discovery tips](https://www.gong.io/blog/here-are-the-11-best-discovery-call-tips-youll-read-this-year)).
