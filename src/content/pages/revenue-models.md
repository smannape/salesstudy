## Evidence

- **SLB Digital** 2025 revenue $2.66bn (+9%), 28% margin; Petrel/Techlog "offered as SaaS subscription or perpetual licenses" ([SLB 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/87347/000119312526021017/slb-20251231.htm)).
- **AVEVA** new licences are subscription-only; Flex credits are pooled; up to 65% lower upfront ([AVEVA blog](https://www.aveva.com/en/perspectives/blog/aveva-accelerates-its-portfolio-wide-commitment-to-subscription-through-aveva-flex/)).
- **AspenTech** token pool — products check tokens out and return them ([AspenTech](https://www.aspentech.com/ja/products/pages/token-licensing-page)).
- **Halliburton iEnergy Stack** — on-prem private cloud delivering DecisionSpace 365 subscription with managed services, positioned for NOCs and data sovereignty ([Halliburton iEnergy Stack](https://www.Halliburton.com/en/software/ienergy/ienergy-stack)).
- **Halliburton–KOC KwIDF** framed as "software and consulting services" with digital-twin design and operation ([Business Wire June 2021](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210616005060/en/Kuwait-Oil-Company-Selects-Halliburton-for-Digital-Transformation-Projects)).
- **AIQ–ADNOC** $340m over 3 years ([JPT](https://jpt.spe.org/aiq-announces-340-million-contract-from-adnoc-for-deployment-of-agentic-ai)).
- **BHGE–NLNG** committed to a 20% trip reduction in three years ([GE](https://www.ge.com/news/press-releases/baker-hughes-ge-company-awarded-contract-nigeria-lng-milestone-digital-agreement-sub)).
- **McKinsey** lists performance-linked contracts (H&P per-well bonuses), SaaS, gain-sharing and risk-sharing, and the barrier "misaligned commercial models, especially where efficiency cuts billable activity" ([OGJ/McKinsey](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/news/55400981/analysis-ai-could-unlock-230-billion-in-annual-upstream-oil-and-gas-value)).

## Seven-model comparison

| Model | How it works | Vendor examples | Pros for NOC | Cons for NOC | Pros for vendor | Cons for vendor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perpetual + annual maintenance | One-off licence fee + annual M&S | SLB Petrel/Techlog (still offered), legacy Landmark | CapEx-friendly; asset on books; no renewal cliff | Large upfront; version lock-in; M&S creep | Upfront cash; sticky base | Lumpy revenue; harder to upsell cloud/AI |
| Term/subscription | Annual or multi-year right-to-use | DecisionSpace 365, Delfi SaaS, AVEVA Flex | Lower entry (AVEVA claims up to 65%); scalable | OpEx budget; renewal risk; cumulative cost | Recurring, "accretive margin" (SLB) | Churn at renewal/tender |
| Token/credit pool | Buy a pool; products draw tokens | AspenTech tokens; AVEVA Flex credits; (SLB Delfi reportedly similar — <span class="tag danger">unverified</span>) | Flexibility across products/regions; usage visibility | Token denials; hard to forecast; audit burden | Portfolio pull-through; consumption upsell | NOC procurement may struggle to specify |
| Enterprise/framework agreement | 3–5 yr umbrella with call-offs | SLB–Petrobras 5-yr; Palantir–bp 5-yr; AIQ–ADNOC 3-yr $340m | One tender covers scale-out; price certainty | Lock-in; big tender effort | Visibility, embedded position | Long cycle, heavy prequalification |
| Services-wrapped software | Software priced inside a services/manpower contract | Halliburton–KOC KwIDF | Fits services templates; adoption support built in | Software value opaque; renewal tied to manpower rates | Easier to tender via services categories | Margin dilution; scope creep |
| Managed service / private cloud in NOC | Vendor operates on-prem private cloud | iEnergy Stack (Petrobel, NOCs); Aramco Digital sovereign cloud | Data residency; limited IT staff needed | Vendor dependency; hardware refresh | Sticky recurring managed-services revenue | Capital/people intensive |
| Outcome / gain-share | Fee tied to uplift, downtime, trips | BHGE–NLNG 20% trip reduction; H&P per-well bonuses | Risk transfer; easy value story | Baseline disputes; audit; procurement rules may not allow | Premium if delivered | Baseline/attribution risk; "efficiency cuts billable activity" |

Framework examples: [SLB–Petrobras](https://www.slb.com/news-and-insights/newsroom/press-release/2023/pr-2023-07-06-petrobras-digital-transformation); [Palantir–bp](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240909534283/en/Palantir-and-bp-Agree-to-5-Year-Strategic-Relationship-With-New-AI-Capabilities); [AIQ–ADNOC](https://jpt.spe.org/aiq-announces-340-million-contract-from-adnoc-for-deployment-of-agentic-ai). Petrobel private cloud: [Halliburton](https://www.halliburton.com/en/about-us/press-release/petrobel-selects-halliburton-landmark-design-deliver-cloud-solutions-ep-applications). Aramco Digital sovereign cloud: [TM Forum](https://inform.tmforum.org/features-and-opinion/aramco-digitals-tareq-amin-spells-out-platform-service-ambitions-for-the-middle-east/).

### What NOC flagship deals look like <span class="tag">inference</span>

NOC flagship deals bundle **platform + hosting (often on-prem/sovereign) + services** in a **multi-year framework**. Pure public-cloud consumption pricing fits committee budgets and data rules poorly.
