## LAMP Gold Sheet structure

Miller Heiman / Korn Ferry Large Account Management Process ([Sybill LAMP](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/miller-heiman-lamp)):

1. **Current account picture** — expected vs achieved outcomes, unproven value, business changes, approaching decisions.
2. **Stakeholder map** — role, influence, stance (supportive/neutral/resistant/unknown), priorities, relationship strength.
3. **Team position and critical vulnerabilities** — e.g. single-champion dependence, no executive relationship.
4. **Charter statement** — measurable, with target outcomes, scope, future position, planning period, mutual value, boundaries; not "trusted strategic partner".
5. **Focus investments** (senior IT/finance access, quantifying adoption impact, a regional pilot) and **STOP investments** (generic demos without validated use case, speculative expansion on weak signals).
6. **Five linked goals** — relationship, customer outcome, strategic initiative, revenue target (e.g. $480K→$900K over 18 months tied to milestones), risk reduction.
7. **Action register** — what, why, owner, participants, expected customer outcome, due date, completion evidence, next decision triggered.

LAMP six elements: segmentation, goal-setting, strategy, execution, buy-in, review ([Kapta LAMP](https://kapta.com/resources/key-account-management-blog/what-is-large-account-management-process-kapta.com); [Korn Ferry LAMP](https://www.kornferry.com/capabilities/leadership-professional-development/training-certifications/large-account-management-process)).

- [ ] Confirmed need
- [ ] Business outcome
- [ ] Engaged stakeholder
- [ ] Feasible solution
- [ ] Decision route
- [ ] Reason to act within a set period
