## Generic enterprise 6-stage model

All percentages <span class="tag warning">indicative</span> — single aggregator; could not be traced to the named reports ([Zeliq](https://www.zeliq.com/blog/sales-cycle)).

| Stage | Conversion to next | Failure point / stall signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Discovery | 55–72% | Prospect redefines scope ("do you also do X?"); silence >14 days |
| 2 Demo | 42–58% (largest drop) | Poorly personalised demo; 67% of deals with no written next step stall |
| 3 Technical validation | 78–88% | Bad sign if the prospect's IT team wasn't asked for |
| 4 Proposal | 60–75%; 7–14 days reply | Committee multiplies as procurement/legal arrive; champion lacks materials |
| 5 Negotiation | 65–80%; 2–4 meetings, 14–30 days | 73% of programmes under-train; 12–18% margin left; unannounced C-level escalation |
| 6 Closing & signature | 70–85%; 7–21 days | Silence >14 days cuts close rate 38% |

- Cumulative discovery→won 16–28%; enterprise median 6–9 months; cycle length 5.2 months (2019) → 7.4 months (2026), +42%; >30% longer than sector benchmark = stalling ([Zeliq](https://www.zeliq.com/blog/sales-cycle)).
- Corroborating: deals >$100k take 6–12+ months; cycles 38% longer than 2021 ([Hyperbound benchmark 2025](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/b2b-sales-performance-benchmark-2025)); Gong: $100k deals ~70 days ([SaaStr/Gong](https://www.saastr.com/gong-100k-deals-take-about-days-to-close)).

## Pre-sales front-loaded view

Research (KYC) → Discovery → Outcomes Definition → Evaluation (Demo/POC) ([Presales Collective](https://presalescollective.com/content/winning-early-the-role-of-the-solution-engineer-early-in-the-sales-cycle)).

- "Most customers select a vendor during Discovery."
- Outcomes definition has "the greatest potential impact on vendor selection."

<div class="checklist" data-checklist="pre-evaluation-checkpoints">

- [ ] Right stakeholders engaged
- [ ] Early fit established
- [ ] Needs and timelines established
- [ ] Competitors and risks known
- [ ] Opportunity worth pursuing

</div>

Checkpoints before evaluation ([Presales Collective](https://presalescollective.com/content/winning-early-the-role-of-the-solution-engineer-early-in-the-sales-cycle)).

## Late stage

- 40–60% of losses are no-decision; treat with JOLT ([Challenger Inc.](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)). See the [JOLT card](/how-software-sales-work/).
- **Mutual Action Plan (MAP):** introduce after qualification, during/after solution presentation, or when stakeholders multiply; work backward from go-live; extend past signature ([GetAccept](https://www.getaccept.com/blog/mutual-action-plans)).
