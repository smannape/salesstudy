## How to read this document

This covers the named, publicly documented digital oilfield (DOF) programmes of Middle East national oil companies, not every digital initiative they run. "Current status" means the last position confirmed by a public source, with its date; where nothing has been published for years, the status is marked as unconfirmed rather than inferred. Each claim carries a confidence tag: <span class="tag success">certain</span> = a primary source was opened; <span class="tag">likely</span> = strong inference from secondary sources; <span class="tag danger">guessing</span> = gap-filling from general knowledge.

## Saudi Arabia and Bahrain

<span class="tag success">certain</span> Aramco no longer runs a programme called "i-Field": the 2004–2017 intelligent-field build-out (19 fields by 2015) has been absorbed into a corporate 4IR / AI programme that Aramco values at $5.3 bn of realised value in 2025, with no external DOF vendor named for any field. Bapco is the opposite — small, and every digital move since 2024 is a named-vendor deal.

## Observations

<span class="tag">likely</span> The region has moved through three contract generations, and KwIDF is the only programme still run on the first one.

1. **Generation 1 (2007–2016): field-by-field DOF pilots with a service-company prime.** KOC KwIDF, Aramco i-Field, PDO Smart Fields, Rumaila ACE. Objective was instrumentation + collaboration centre + workflow automation, with 3–7% production uplift as the KPI. These are the only programmes that ever published well counts.
2. **Generation 2 (2017–2023): enterprise command centres and NOC-owned platforms.** ADNOC Panorama/Thamama, Aramco 4IR Center, PDO IOC. The prime shifted from service companies to software/automation vendors (AVEVA, Honeywell, Emerson) or in-house teams, and the KPI became "$ value realised" — a number only the NOC can audit.
3. **Generation 3 (2024–2026): AI/agentic layers procured as multi-year platform contracts, with the digital scope bundled into drilling, field-management or EPC awards.** ENERGYai ($340 M), AiPSO, RTOC, PDO autonomous programme, KBR Majnoon, Halliburton Bin Umar/Sindbad, Jereh ($920 M). The service company returns as prime, but as integrator of an AI stack (SLB Lumi, Landmark DS365) over an NOC-owned data layer (Cognite, OSDU, sovereign cloud).

Patterns worth noting:

- **Where programmes went quiet.** ADNOC Panorama (no value update since 2021), ICM (2023), RoboWell (no well count since the Jul 2024 300-well target), Aramco CNTXT (May 2024), Bapco–Palantir (Feb 2024). Silence after a launch is the norm, not the exception; absence of news is <span class="tag danger">guessing</span> either way.
- **Values are published only when the award is an EPC or a headline AI deal.** Of 22 programmes, four have a public value. Service-company digital contracts (Halliburton/Weatherford KwIDF 2021, SLB AiPSO/RTOC, Kongsberg PDO) are consistently undisclosed.
- **Sovereignty is now a stated requirement.** ADNOC RTOC runs in sovereign cloud; Bapco retains raw data and trained models; Oman's MEM data-residency rules shaped CCED's cloud deal; Aramco's Microsoft MoU is explicitly "sovereign Azure". Any next-generation DOF contract will carry this clause.
- **The NOC-owned AI vendor is a UAE-only model.** AIQ (ADNOC 49%) is prime on every ADNOC upstream AI product and now exports; no other NOC has replicated it, though Aramco's HUMAIN stake points the same way.
- **Drilling automation is where SLB has consolidated** (ADNOC 120 rigs, PDO 50 rigs, Qatar, Turnwell); production-side AI is still contested between SLB, Halliburton and AIQ.

Benchmarks against KwIDF <span class="tag">likely</span>: KwIDF's >5,000 wells exceeds any single published ADNOC or Aramco well count, but its public KPI (3–5% uplift) is Generation-1 language while peers now report AI use-case counts, $ value and autonomous-operations targets. The nearest structural comparators for a re-tendered KwIDF are PDO's Oct 2026 autonomous programme (one NOC, SLB + Halliburton jointly) and ADNOC's AiPSO (SLB prime, Cognite data layer, 25 fields by 2027).
