## Planning structure

<span class="tag">inference</span>

1. **3-year account charter (LAMP)** per key account — see [Target a client](/target-a-client/).
2. **Annual V2MOM** for the territory.
3. **Quarterly OKRs** (2–3).
4. **Weekly MEDDPICC pipeline review** — see [Metrics](/metrics/).
5. **QBR/EBR** with the customer.

Tie objectives to the customer's tender calendar and budget cycle, not vendor quarters.

## V2MOM

<div class="panel">

<p class="eyebrow">Card</p>

### V2MOM

- **Vision** — specific outcome for a period.
- **Values** — ranked.
- **Methods** — ranked, owned.
- **Obstacles** — ranked, each with a resolution.
- **Measures** — baseline + target.

Annual, cascaded, living ([Rework V2MOM](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/strategic-management/v2mom-framework); [Lattice](https://lattice.com/articles/v2moms-how-they-work-and-when-to-use-them)).

**Example sales V2MOM**

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Vision | "first-choice vendor… this year" |
| Values | customer fit over deal size, forecast honesty, speed to first meeting |
| Methods | rebuild ICP from closed-won data, discovery checklist, referral programme |
| Obstacles | inconsistent CRM data, long procurement cycles, rep turnover |
| Measures | qualified pipeline created, win rate by segment, average cycle length |

</div>

## OKRs

"KPIs monitor the same thing constantly… OKRs take you somewhere new, and they're temporary." Limit to 2–3 objectives per quarter; key results are metrics and within your influence ([Perdoo](https://www.perdoo.com/resources/online-guides/20-okr-examples-for-sales)).

Example key results:

- NRR 105% → 120%
- Solutions per customer 1.5 → 2.3
- Qualified-opp-to-close 25% → 40%
- Enterprise deal size +35%
- Business reviews 35% → 60%

## Expansion economics

<span class="tag warning">indicative</span> SaaS, not O&G.

- Enterprise (ACV >$100K) median NRR 118%; CAC payback 18–24 months; remaining lifetime should be ≥2x payback (Optifai benchmark, 939 SaaS companies) ([Rework CAC payback](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/growth-frameworks/cac-payback-optimization)).
- Bowtie model extends the funnel to retention/expansion; "Dedicated Touch" = one team on one large account ([Winning by Design](https://winningbydesign.com/press/winning-by-design-releases-updated-bowtie-model-and-benchmarks-to-provide-go-to-market-teams-with-a-standardized-customer-journey-and-data-model/)).
